TERM ID: TERM-MRP-EXPLOSION
TERM: MRP explosion
DEFINITION: Running MRP for Level 1 (the garment) for the full quantity also generates the indents for the child level (the raw materials of the BOM) in the same run: child quantity = Level 1 quantity × BOM Final Consumption. If the parent changes later, re-running MRP carries the change into the child indent.

TERM ID: TERM-MRP-ADJUSTMENT
TERM: MRP adjustment
DEFINITION: Every MRP level (parent and child) can take a negative quantity (for example -500). The level where you enter it is the level that changes. To reduce or cancel a child indent, the negative has to be entered at its parent level: that lowers the parent quantity MRP acts on, so once MRP is re-run the child indent is reduced by the same share (any change in the parent followed by a re-run of MRP impacts the child indent), and taking off the whole parent quantity (-500 of 500) cancels it.

TERM ID: TERM-NEGATIVE-AT-THE-CHILD-LEVEL
TERM: Negative at the child level
DEFINITION: A negative quantity entered at a child level changes only that child level. It does not reach the parent and does not cancel the child indent by itself: cancelling a child indent needs the negative at the parent level.

TERM: Price Per

TERM ID: TERM-UPCHARGE-DISCOUNT
TERM: Upcharge / Discount
DEFINITION: Driven by Price Per. The base selling price comes from the Tech Pack. A positive value is an upcharge, a negative value is a discount, and the final selling price is read-only: Base Selling Price ± Upcharge/Discount. Only when the setting "Enable Base Price and Upcharge in Summary OC" is ON; when OFF the Tech Pack price goes straight into Selling Price.
