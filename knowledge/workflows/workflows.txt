STEP DETAILS:
  masters: Colours, sizes and units that every techpack and BOM line must use.
  rm: One techpack per raw material: fabric N-8 and three trims – sewing thread, cufflinks and polybag.
  style: The Adi TShirt (NIS-59) for GAP Kids, using colour card Nis_colors and size range ERPSIZE.
  bom: 4 lines: fabric N-8 1.65 KG, thread 2.04 m, cufflinks 2.04, polybag 1 per T-shirt.
  routing: VCutting → Sewing → VPacking, used for every colour and size.
  operations: Each operation has a machine, a Cost Of and a production time; together they give the process rate.
  linkage: Each BOM line is mapped to the process that consumes it.
  costing: Total cost 25,115.72 against selling price 4,000 less 10 % discount.
  approve: Adi TShirt (NIS-59) #1 DP moved from Open to Approved; only approved BOMs appear in the block-booking BOM dropdown. Key point: Only approved BOMs are shown in the BBB BOM dropdown.
  bbb-create: A projection for GAP Kids, season AW26 – before any confirmed order.
  bbb-details: Booking line PPJ179.1 with 100 per colour/size, delivery date and product group.
  bbb-article: The article the booking is for. The BOM list is filtered by it.
  bbb-bom: Pick an approved BOM; "Add From Catalog" is hidden once a BOM is selected. Key point: Add From Catalog is hidden when a BOM is selected.
  bbb-populate: WFX copies every material of the BOM into the booking. Key point: Units column editability depends on Auto MRP configuration.
  bbb-materials: Requirement per colour/size = 100 × 1 = 100 KG; total 600 KG. Key point: Requirement % is calculated automatically.
  bbb-save: Saved: 600 KG of fabric, value 3,000.
  gen-indent: The user clicks "Generate Indent Requirement" on the booking.
  adhoc: Adhoc Indent with every BOM material, requirement 600 KG and the BBB reference.
  po: PO to the supplier for 600 KG at 10 = 6,000. (only after the booking indent reaches PO)
  asn: Supplier ships 600 KG (advance shipping notice). (only after the booking indent reaches ASN)
  grn: 600 KG accepted into ACHIEVER CUT STORE, tagged PPJ179. (only after the booking indent reaches GRN)
  oc: 1,000 pcs by colour × size with BOM #1 DP selected.
  link: PPJ179/1 is linked while creating the sales order and the order is confirmed.
  mrp: Level 1: 1,000 pcs to make. Level 2: 1,000 × 1.5 = 1,500 KG to buy.
  mfg-indent: Asks production to make 1,000 pcs.
  so-indent: Asks purchasing for 1,500 KG of fabric – before adjustment.
  adjust: In-House Adjustment of 400 KG at SKU level.
  so-updated: 1,500 KG − 400 KG adjusted = 1,100 KG still to buy.
  rmpo-gap: Purchase order only for the 1,100 KG not covered by the booking.
  prod-order: Decides how much of each colour is released to the factory (Qty %).
  fpo: 1,000 pcs released; fabric 1,500 KG: 400 KG ready, 1,100 KG open.
  fppo: Each routing process gets 1,000 pcs to produce.
