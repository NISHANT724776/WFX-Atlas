PROCEDURE ID: PROC-INVENTORY-PRODUCTION-MATERIAL-ISSUE
TITLE: How is material issued to production?
TOPIC: Production material issue
MODULE: Inventory, QC & WMS
SOURCE DOCUMENT: WMS, Inventory and QC process flow and To-Be
CONTENT: The production team raises a GDI (Goods Dispatch Instruction) to the inventory team against the production order. The inventory team prepares the goods and creates a pick list. Several pick lists can be created against one GDI depending on the physical stock. When the pick list is final, the inventory team creates the GDN (Goods Dispatch Note), and the system reduces the dispatched stock from inventory.
STEPS:
  1. Production raises a GDI against the production order.
  2. The inventory team prepares the goods and creates a pick list.
  3. Multiple pick lists can be generated against one GDI.
  4. The inventory team creates the GDN.
  5. The system automatically reduces the dispatched stock.

PROCEDURE ID: PROC-INVENTORY-WMS-PREREQUISITES
TITLE: What must be configured before using WMS?
TOPIC: WMS prerequisites
MODULE: Inventory, QC & WMS
SOURCE DOCUMENT: WMS Workflow
CONTENT: Some masters must be enabled from report rights and the QR/barcode master set up for the transactions that need a QR code. Quick Docs must be enabled from the Translation Configurator for every transaction to be done on the mobile app.
STEPS:
  1. Enable the QR/Barcode master and the Stationary master from report rights.
  2. Give the scan option for GRN and GDI (permission) and the duplicate print permission.
  3. Enable Roll ASN Against Movement Article (Inventory setting > Company Setup).
  4. Enable Site location in report rights, to generate QR codes for sites.
  5. Define the QR code / barcode master setup: for which transaction a QR code is generated.
  6. Enable Quick Docs from the Translation Configurator, for each transaction to be done on mobile.
WHERE IN WFX:
NOTES: Build 2025_01 must be deployed on the server. The mobile app is installed from the WFX QuickDocs APK (WFXQuickDocs_0407_01.apk).

PROCEDURE ID: PROC-INVENTORY-WMS-GRN-STEPS
TITLE: How do I make a GRN from the mobile application?
TOPIC: WMS GRN steps
MODULE: Inventory, QC & WMS
SOURCE DOCUMENT: WMS Workflow
CONTENT: Click GRN, select the receipt type, site and supplier, enter the ASN number, FPPO number, GIN number, PRPO number or GDN number as the receipt type requires, then scan the QR from the source and make the GRN.
STEPS:
  1. Click GRN.
  2. Select the receipt type.
  3. Select the site.
  4. Select the supplier.
  5. Enter the ASN / FPPO / GIN / PRPO / GDN number, as per the receipt type.
  6. Scan the QR from the source and do the GRN.
WHERE IN WFX:
NOTES: For a GRN against an RMPO, use the supplier portal or create a supplier ASN against the RMPO so that the QR code can be generated, then scan it. Without an ASN, do the GRN on the web screen; the QR code is generated there, can be printed and attached to the rolls, and is used during production. Roll ASN Against Movement Article must be enabled to generate the QR on the ASN.

PROCEDURE ID: PROC-INVENTORY-ROLL-FUNCTIONALITY-FABRIC-ROLL-TRACKING-ROLL-WISE-STOCK-ROLL
TITLE: How does roll functionality work in WFX and why is it used?
TOPIC: Roll functionality (fabric roll tracking, roll-wise stock, roll QR code, traceability)
MODULE: Inventory, QC & WMS
SOURCE DOCUMENT: WMS, Inventory and QC process flow and To-Be
CONTENT: In WFX fabric is tracked roll by roll, not only as a total quantity. The GRN captures roll details with the colour, size, lot and shade, and a QR code is generated for the rolls. That one code follows the roll through the whole flow: QC, location changes, allocation to an indent, issue to production (GDI pick list and GDN), Smart Scan and splitting. It is used because a fabric roll is the real physical unit: its shade, lot and length differ from roll to roll, so stock by total quantity alone is not enough to find, allocate, inspect or issue the right roll.
STEPS:
  1. Receive: the GRN (or the supplier ASN) captures the roll details and generates the QR code; print it and attach it to the roll.
  2. Check: if QC is selected on the GRN, the roll stock is usable only after QC; rejected rolls create a debit note.
  3. Store: scan the roll QR and then the QR of the new location to change its location.
  4. Allocate: the indent overview shows the requested roll quantity for roll allocation.
  5. Issue: production raises a GDI; the inventory team scans the rolls to build the pick list, then makes the GDN.
  6. Inspect or split: Smart Scan shows the full details of a roll and lets you split its quantity.
WHERE IN WFX: Inventory > GRN, Stock Management, WFX Stock Movement list; mobile app: GRN, Location, Dispatch, Smart Scan
NOTES: Setting needed: Roll ASN Against Movement Article (Inventory setting > Company Setup) and the QR/Barcode master. The manuals describe how roll handling works; they do not give a separate written reason, so the reasons above are read from what each step does. Ask the WFX team if you need the official business reason.
