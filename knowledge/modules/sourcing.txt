PROCEDURE ID: PROC-SOURCING-SOURCING-STANDARD-FLOW
TITLE: What is the standard sourcing flow?
TOPIC: Sourcing standard flow
MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SOURCE DOCUMENT: Sourcing Module User Manual
CONTENT: The article is created in the Catalog, used in the BOM, an order triggers MRP, the indent is reviewed, and the requirement is met from stock or by an RMPO.
STEPS:
  1. Create or verify the material article in the Catalog, with its colours and sizes.
  2. Make sure the article is in the BOM with consumption, wastage, price and supplier details, colour/size dependency and the purchase officer.
  3. Generate MRP after the sales order exists and review the indent in the User Indent List.
  4. Open the indent and check the required quantity, material attributes and available stock.
  5. If suitable stock exists use the in-house or equivalent-stock process; otherwise create an RMPO.
  6. Complete the mandatory RMPO fields and verify quantity, price, supplier, dates, currency and UOM conversion.
  7. Follow the RMPO status and track PO, GRN, receipt and dispatch quantities.

PROCEDURE ID: PROC-SOURCING-CREATE-RMPO
TITLE: How do I create a Raw Material Purchase Order (RMPO)?
TOPIC: Create RMPO
MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SOURCE DOCUMENT: Sourcing Module User Manual
CONTENT: Go to Sourcing > My Purchase Order > New Raw Material Purchase Order. The screen has Header, Article Detail and Additional Charges sections. Fields in red are mandatory.
STEPS:
  1. Select the category (for example Textiles/Fabric).
  2. Select the supplier.
  3. Enter the required delivery and in-house dates.
  4. Select the currency and complete the other mandatory header fields.
  5. Enter or review the article details and verify the quantity against the indent.
  6. Check the UOM and conversion, add additional charges if applicable, and create or confirm the order.

PROCEDURE ID: PROC-SOURCING-RMPO-FOR-MULTIPLE-INDENTS
TITLE: How do I create one RMPO for multiple indents together?
TOPIC: RMPO for multiple indents
MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SOURCE DOCUMENT: Sourcing Module User Manual
CONTENT: From the User Indent List, several indents of the same category can be combined into one RMPO. Pick the indents together, start the purchase order from the list, and the RMPO screen opens for the combined requirement.
STEPS:
  1. Open Sourcing > Indent Management > User Indent List.
  2. Pick the indents to combine; they must be of the same category (for example all Textiles/Fabric).
  3. Start the RMPO from the list. The RMPO screen opens with the Header, Article Detail and Additional Charges sections.
  4. Complete the mandatory header fields (shown in red): category, supplier, delivery and in-house dates and currency.
  5. In Article Detail check the quantity, price, PO UOM and UOM conversion against each indent.
  6. Create or confirm the RMPO. If the Supplier Portal is on, it stays Unconfirmed until the supplier responds.
WHERE IN WFX: Sourcing > Indent Management > User Indent List
NOTES: Only indents of the same category can be combined into one RMPO. As the manual says, screen options and fields can vary by user role, configuration and module version.

PROCEDURE ID: PROC-SOURCING-SUPPLIER-PORTAL
TITLE: How does the supplier portal work for an RMPO?
TOPIC: Supplier portal
MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SOURCE DOCUMENT: Sourcing Module User Manual
CONTENT: If the supplier has a login and the Supplier Portal feature is enabled, the RMPO is sent to the supplier portal for approval and may stay Unconfirmed until the supplier responds. PROCEDURE ID: PROC-SOURCING-INDENT-FULFILLMENT-STATUS
TITLE: What do the quantities on Indent Fulfillment Status mean, and how do I track dispatch?
TOPIC: Indent fulfillment status
MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SOURCE DOCUMENT: Sourcing Module User Manual
CONTENT: It is the main screen to monitor how indent requirements are fulfilled. Select the buyer (and buyer department and season) and click Search.
STEPS:
WHERE IN WFX:
NOTES: Open Qty = quantity still open. Order Qty = covered by purchase orders. PO GRN Receipt Qty = received through GRN against the PO.
