RULE NAME: Level 1 Req Qty (garment)
MODULE: Planning
SUBMODULE:
TRANSACTION: MRP – Material Requirement Planning

OUTPUT: Req Qty

RULE ID: CALC-MRP-ADJUST
RULE NAME: MRP adjustment (negative quantity)
MODULE: Planning
SUBMODULE:
TRANSACTION: MRP – Material Requirement Planning
RULE CATEGORY: Calculation
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Every MRP level can take a negative quantity. A child indent is reduced or cancelled only by a negative at its parent level, and it takes effect when MRP is re-run: any change in the parent followed by a re-run of MRP impacts the child indent. Taking off the whole parent quantity (-500 of 500) cancels it.
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
  Parent Qty
  Adjustment (negative reduces)
  BOM Final Consumption
FORMULA / LOGIC: Entered at the parent: Net Parent Qty = Parent Qty + Adjustment; Child Indent Qty = Net Parent Qty × BOM Final Consumption. Entered at a child level: only that level changes (Child Qty + Adjustment).
UNIT OF MEASUREMENT:
ROUNDING RULE:
OUTPUT: Entered at the parent: Net Parent Qty
VALIDATIONS:
EXAMPLE: Level 1 = 500, Final Consumption 1.5: no adjustment → 750; -200 at Level 1, re-run MRP → 300 × 1.5 = 450; -500 at Level 1, re-run MRP → 0 (cancelled); -200 at Level 2 → fabric 550, Level 1 stays 500.
EXPECTED RESULT:
EXCEPTION CONDITIONS:
RELATED TRANSACTIONS:
RELATED RULES:
CALC INPUTS:
  parent_qty | Parent Qty | quantity | required
  adjustment | Adjustment (negative reduces) | quantity | default 0
  final_consumption | BOM Final Consumption | quantity | required
EXPRESSION:
  net_parent_qty = parent_qty + adjustment
  child_indent_qty = net_parent_qty * final_consumption
CALC OUTPUT: child_indent_qty
