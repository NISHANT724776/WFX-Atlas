RULE ID: CALC-FPO-ALLOC-OPEN
RULE NAME: Material Allocation Open Qty
MODULE: Production
SUBMODULE:
TRANSACTION: FPO & FPPO
RULE CATEGORY: Calculation
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Qty Instructed is stock already reserved (the BBB in-house adjustment).
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
  Qty Reqd
  Qty Instructed
  Qty Unreserved
FORMULA / LOGIC: Open Qty = Qty Reqd − Net Qty Instructed (Qty Instructed − Qty Unreserved)
UNIT OF MEASUREMENT:
ROUNDING RULE:
OUTPUT: Open Qty
VALIDATIONS:
EXAMPLE: 150 − 100 = 50; total 1,100 = RMPO 1230 qty.
EXPECTED RESULT:
EXCEPTION CONDITIONS:
RELATED TRANSACTIONS:
RELATED RULES:
CALC INPUTS:
  qty_reqd | Qty Reqd | quantity | required
  qty_instructed | Qty Instructed | quantity | default 0
  qty_unreserved | Qty Unreserved | quantity | default 0
EXPRESSION: open_qty = qty_reqd - (qty_instructed - qty_unreserved)
CALC OUTPUT: open_qty
