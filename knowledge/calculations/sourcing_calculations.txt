RULE ID: CALC-INDENT-OPEN
RULE NAME: Open Qty & Status
MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SUBMODULE:
TRANSACTION: BBB Adhoc Indent
RULE CATEGORY: Calculation
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Open qty is the part of the indent not yet covered by a PO or by stock.
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
  Indent Qty
  Ordered Qty
  In-house Adj.
  On Order Adj.
FORMULA / LOGIC: Open Qty = Indent Qty − Ordered Qty − In-house Adj. − On Order Adj.; Status = Completed when Open Qty = 0
UNIT OF MEASUREMENT:
ROUNDING RULE:
OUTPUT: Open Qty
VALIDATIONS:
EXAMPLE: 100 − 100 − 0 − 0 = 0 → Completed.
EXPECTED RESULT:
EXCEPTION CONDITIONS:
RELATED TRANSACTIONS:
RELATED RULES:
CALC INPUTS:
  indent_qty | Indent Qty | quantity | required
  ordered_qty | Ordered Qty | quantity | default 0
  in_house_adj | In-house Adj. | quantity | default 0
  on_order_adj | On Order Adj. | quantity | default 0
EXPRESSION: open_qty = indent_qty - ordered_qty - in_house_adj - on_order_adj

MODULE: Booking & sourcing
SUBMODULE:

UNIT OF MEASUREMENT:
ROUNDING RULE:

VALIDATIONS:

EXPECTED RESULT:
EXCEPTION CONDITIONS:
RELATED TRANSACTIONS:
RELATED RULES:

  surcharge_value | Surcharge Value | money | default 0
EXPRESSION:
