RULE ID: RULE-MRP-ADJUSTMENT
RULE NAME: MRP adjustment
MODULE: Planning
SUBMODULE: MRP
TRANSACTION: MRP
RULE CATEGORY: Business Rule
BUSINESS PURPOSE:
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
FORMULA / LOGIC: Every MRP level (parent and child) can take a negative quantity (for example -500). The level where you enter it is the level that changes. To reduce or cancel a child indent, the negative has to be entered at its parent level: that lowers the parent quantity MRP acts on, so once MRP is re-run the child indent is reduced by the same share (any change in the parent followed by a re-run of MRP impacts the child indent), and taking off the whole parent quantity (-500 of 500) cancels it.
UNIT OF MEASUREMENT:
ROUNDING RULE:
OUTPUT:
VALIDATIONS:
EXAMPLE: Level 1 = 500. Negative -200 at Level 1: net 300, child = 300 × Final Consumption. Negative -500 at Level 1: net 0, child indent cancelled.
