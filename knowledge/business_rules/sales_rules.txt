RULE NAME: Upcharge / Discount
MODULE: Sales Order
SUBMODULE: Sales Order (re-write)
TRANSACTION: Sales Order (OC)
RULE CATEGORY: Pricing
BUSINESS PURPOSE:
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
FORMULA / LOGIC: Driven by Price Per. The base selling price comes from the Tech Pack. A positive value is an upcharge, a negative value is a discount, and the final selling price is read-only: Base Selling Price ± Upcharge/Discount. Only when the setting "Enable Base Price and Upcharge in Summary OC" is ON; when OFF the Tech Pack price goes straight into Selling Price.

RULE NAME: Extra Production % and Additional Quantity
MODULE: Sales Order
SUBMODULE: Sales Order (re-write)
TRANSACTION: Sales Order (OC)
RULE CATEGORY: Quantity
BUSINESS PURPOSE:
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
FORMULA / LOGIC: Extra Production % entered at the article header adds a percentage on top of the order quantity and is shown in its own grid under the Quantity Breakup on the Proforma Invoice and Print page. Additional Quantity is added in a separate tab with its own quantity, selling price and a reason from a dropdown. RULE ID: RULE-SO-PRICE-PER
RULE NAME: Price Per
MODULE: Sales Order
SUBMODULE: Sales Order (re-write)
TRANSACTION: Sales Order (OC)
RULE CATEGORY: Pricing
BUSINESS PURPOSE:
TRIGGER CONDITION:
APPLICABLE CONDITIONS:
INPUT FIELDS:
FORMULA / LOGIC: Inherited from the Tech Pack, never typed on the order. It decides at which level the selling price is kept: Article (one price), Color (a price per colour, average shown), Size (a price per size, average shown) or Color/Size (a price per combination, average shown). What each type does to the delivery-level grid of the Sales Order / Purchase Order from Buyer: Article – the grid is formed article-wise. Color – the grid is formed colour-wise; upcharges and discounts are handled vertically. Size – the same as Color, but upcharges, discounts and fields are handled horizontally. Color/Size – the grid works like Article, but colour-wise.
