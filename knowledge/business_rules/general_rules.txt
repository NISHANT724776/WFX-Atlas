TRANSACTION: BOM – Materials & Dependency (bom-materials)
RULE CATEGORY: Business Rule
BUSINESS PURPOSE: The BOM is organised in the sections of the cost sheet template.
FORMULA / LOGIC: The sections of a BOM are derived from the cost sheet template. In the template the categories are defined, such as fabric, trims, yarn and miscellaneous. For each material line the Purchase UOM and the Cons UOM are defined; the colours and sizes that are needed are added and mapped according to the dependency selected.
RELATED RULES: RULE-SETUP-BOM-MASTER-COST, RULE-SETUP-UOM-PURCHASE

RULE ID: RULE-BOM-ROUTING-PROCESS-SETUP
RULE NAME: Routing: how a process is defined
MODULE: Style & BOM
TRANSACTION: BOM – Routing (bom-routing)
RULE CATEGORY: Business Rule
BUSINESS PURPOSE: The routing lists the processes that produce the garment, in order and costed.
FORMULA / LOGIC: On the Routing page of the BOM the user defines the processes that produce the apparel (for example cutting, stitching, sewing, packing). For each process the user defines the input item and the output item, the After Process (the process that comes before it: the process with none is the first), and a Process Rate.
RELATED RULES: CALC-ROUTING-AFTER-VALID, CALC-ROUTING-PROCESS-RATE
SCREEN / LOCATION: BOM › Routing

RULE ID: RULE-BOM-ROUTING-NORMAL-MATERIAL-FLOW
RULE NAME: Routing: a normal material flow – which materials go to cutting, sewing and packing
MODULE: Style & BOM
TRANSACTION: BOM – Routing (bom-routing)
RULE CATEGORY: Business Rule
BUSINESS PURPOSE: The routing maps raw materials to the processes that use them.
FORMULA / LOGIC: In a normal flow: the fabric goes through cutting; then the fabric, thread and the other items go to sewing; and the final garment with the polybag goes to packing. The owner gave this as the example that runs a normal flow without hindrance.
RELATED RULES: CALC-ROUTING-LINKAGE
SCREEN / LOCATION: BOM › Routing (material linkage)
EXAMPLE: Cutting: fabric · Sewing: fabric, thread, other items · Packing: final garment with polybag.
