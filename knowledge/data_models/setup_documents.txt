MODEL ID: DM-BOM-MATERIALS
MODEL NAME: BOM · Materials
MODEL LEVEL: Captured screen fields (confirmed from WFX screens/specifications; not database schema)
CHAPTER: Style setup – Adi TShirt (NIS-59) · captured from live WFX screens
SCREEN: Apparel › BOM › Materials
SUMMARY: One line per material: how much one T-shirt consumes, with wastage, and what it costs. Value flows to the Cost tab; each line is linked to a process on the Routing tab.
HEADER FIELDS:
  Article | copied | from: apparel.value
  BOM | entered
  Version | key
  Status | entered
  Currency / Date | entered
  Order Qty Expected / Actual | entered
GRIDS:
  [Materials (4)] (4 captured rows)
  Line | key
  Article Code | link | from: rm-techpack.code
  Article Name | copied | from: rm-techpack.name
  Purchase UOM | entered
  Cons UOM | copied | from: rm-techpack.consUom
  Material Colors | entered | note: Colour dependency (Table): each style colour is mapped to a material colour from the Nis_colors colour card. Click a cell to see the mapping.
  Material Sizes | entered | note: Size dependency: each style size is mapped to a material size from the ERPSIZE size range. Click a cell to see the mapping.
  Total Cons Qty | entered
  Cons per Item | entered
  Cons Qty | calc | formula: Total Cons Qty ÷ Cons per Item | rule: CALC-BOM-CONS-QTY
  Loss % | entered | note: Applied first: Cons × 1 ÷ (1 − Loss %).
  Cons incl Loss | calc | formula: Cons Qty × 1 ÷ (1 − Loss %) | rule: CALC-BOM-CONS-LOSS
  Wastage % | entered
  Cons incl Wastage | calc | formula: Cons incl Loss + (Cons incl Loss × Wastage %) | rule: CALC-BOM-CONS-WASTAGE
  Shrinkage % | entered | note: Applied last: Cons incl Wastage ÷ (1 − Shrinkage %).
  Final Consumption | calc | formula: Cons incl Wastage ÷ (1 − Shrinkage %)  (the total consumption) | rule: CALC-BOM-FINAL-CONS
  Supplier | entered
  Purchase Price | entered
  Conversion | copied | note: Purchase → Cons UOM factor from UOM Conversion; 1 when none is maintained.
  Cons UOM Price | calc | formula: Purchase Price × Conversion | rule: CALC-BOM-CONS-UOM-PRICE
  Value | calc | formula: Final Consumption × Cons UOM Price | rule: CALC-BOM-VALUE
  [Consumption chain – worked example (Cons 2)] (4 captured rows)
  Case | key
  Cons Qty | entered
  Loss % | entered
  Wastage % | entered
  Shrinkage % | entered
  Loss Factor | calc | formula: 1 ÷ (1 − Loss %) | rule: CALC-BOM-LOSS-FACTOR
  Cons incl Loss | calc | formula: Cons × Loss Factor | rule: CALC-BOM-CONS-LOSS
  Cons incl Wastage | calc | formula: Cons incl Loss + Cons incl Loss × Wastage % | rule: CALC-BOM-CONS-WASTAGE
  Total Consumption | calc | formula: Cons incl Wastage ÷ (1 − Shrinkage %) | rule: CALC-BOM-FINAL-CONS
