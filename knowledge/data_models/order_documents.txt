MODEL ID: DM-MRP
MODEL NAME: MRP
MODEL LEVEL: Captured screen fields (confirmed from WFX screens/specifications; not database schema)
CHAPTER: Booking, order & production – Flannel Shirt Adi (NIS-58) · from the Excel trial
SCREEN: Planning › MRP
SUMMARY: Level 1 = garments to make (from the OC). Level 2 = fabric to buy: garment qty × BOM final consumption. Running Level 1 for the full qty generates the child indents. Any level can take a negative, but a child indent is reduced or cancelled only by a negative at its parent level, and only after MRP is re-run.
HEADER FIELDS:
  Order | copied
  Triggered by | calc
GRIDS:
  [Level 1 – Garment (4)] (4 captured rows)
  L1 Key | key
  OC Line | link | from: oc.key
  Actual Requirement | copied | from: oc.qty
  MRP % | entered
  Req Qty | calc | formula: Actual Requirement × MRP % × (1 + Loss %) | rule: CALC-MRP-L1-REQ
  Demand Type | calc | rule: CALC-MRP-DEMAND
  [Level 2 – Fabric N-8 (4)] (4 captured rows)
  L2 Key | key
  Parent L1 | link | from: mrp.key
  Garment Req Qty | copied | from: mrp.reqQty
  Final Consumption | copied | from: bom-58.finalCons
  Req Qty | calc | formula: Garment Req Qty × Final Consumption | rule: CALC-MRP-L2-REQ
  Demand Type | calc | rule: CALC-MRP-DEMAND
  [Adjustment scenarios (Level 1 = 500)] (4 captured rows)
  Entered at | entered | note: Every level can take a negative; only one entered at the parent level changes the child indent.
  MRP Adjustment | entered | note: Negative quantity the user enters at MRP.
  Level 1 Qty | copied
  Net Level 1 Qty | calc | formula: Level 1 Qty + Adjustment (when entered at Level 1) | rule: CALC-MRP-ADJUST
  Final Consumption | copied | from: bom-58.finalCons
  Child Indent Qty | calc | formula: Net Level 1 Qty × Final Consumption (+ Adjustment when entered at Level 2) | rule: CALC-MRP-ADJUST
  Result | calc | note: Full quantity generates the child indent; part reduces it; the whole quantity cancels it.
