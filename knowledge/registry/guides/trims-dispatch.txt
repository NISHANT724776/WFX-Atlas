KEYWORDS: trims, scan, QR, Quick Docs, mobile, Pick List, Dispatch trims, pick list, picking
SUMMARY: Six flows in the Quick Docs app, all built around scanning: receiving trims into stock (GRN), picking them for dispatch (Pick List), issuing them on a delivery note (GDN), moving them between locations, printing packaging movement QR labels from the phone, and the outward note types (production inputs, returns and transfers). Pick a flow, then try it on the phone. The camera is simulated, so tap a label to hold it up to the lens.
WHAT CHANGES: • Scanning a packaging movement shows exactly which article, colour and size it holds, so nothing is picked blind.
  • Pick quantity fills itself with what is required or what the container holds, whichever is less.
  • A packaging movement without the article or colour is rejected on the spot.
DATA NOTE: The prototype uses sample data only. It demonstrates the intended Quick Docs flow; it is not a live WFX transaction.

STEP 4 TEXT: The GDI lists each article with its required quantity, what is already dispatched and what is still pending. The pick quantity can never go above the pending quantity. Tap an article to pick for it, and tap Create Pick List when done. After the GDN, a new Pick List can be created for what is still pending, until it reaches 0.
