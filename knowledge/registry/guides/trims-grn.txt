SUMMARY: Six flows in the Quick Docs app, all built around scanning: receiving trims into stock (GRN), picking them for dispatch (Pick List), issuing them on a delivery note (GDN), moving them between locations, printing packaging movement QR labels from the phone, and the outward note types (production inputs, returns and transfers). Pick a flow, then try it on the phone. The camera is simulated, so tap a label to hold it up to the lens.
WHAT CHANGES: • Supplier, PO, document number and date arrive from the ASN QR. Nothing to type.
  • Every scanned packaging movement number becomes a row, and the article quantity is split equally across the rows by default.
  • Quantity can be lowered but never raised above what is left to receive. Wrong-article and repeat scans are blocked.
DATA NOTE: The prototype uses sample data only. It demonstrates the intended Quick Docs flow; it is not a live WFX transaction.

STEP 7 TITLE: Add packaging movement numbers
STEP 7 TEXT: Scan the PMN QR, or pick the number from the master by hand: name and UOM fill in, then enter the pack quantity and tap Add. A packaging movement that does not exist yet can be created on the spot, and the app offers to print its label. Each add creates a row, and the quantity is split equally across the rows (1,000 over 2 packs gives 500 and 500). Every row stays editable, never above what is left to receive.
