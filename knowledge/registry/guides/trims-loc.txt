KEYWORDS: trims, scan, QR, Quick Docs, mobile, Location, Move stock, location, move stock, bin
SUMMARY: Six flows in the Quick Docs app, all built around scanning: receiving trims into stock (GRN), picking them for dispatch (Pick List), issuing them on a delivery note (GDN), moving them between locations, printing packaging movement QR labels from the phone, and the outward note types (production inputs, returns and transfers). Pick a flow, then try it on the phone. The camera is simulated, so tap a label to hold it up to the lens.
WHAT CHANGES: • One scan shows every article inside a packaging movement, with colour, size and total quantity, so nothing has to be looked up.
  • Moving part of an article is simple: pick the quantity, scan the new location, and put it in a packaging movement there, created on the spot if none exists.
  • The system books each quantity to the new location. Over-moving is blocked and a packaging movement from another site is rejected.
DATA NOTE: The prototype uses sample data only. It demonstrates the intended Quick Docs flow; it is not a live WFX transaction.

STEP 7 TITLE: Put the stock in a packaging movement
STEP 7 TEXT: For a partial move, scan or choose a packaging movement for the new location: an empty one, or one already at that location. If none exists, create a new one on the spot. A whole-package move skips this step.

STEP 8 TITLE: Update and repeat
STEP 8 TEXT: Update Location moves a whole package to the new location, or books the quantities into the chosen packaging movement there. The source keeps the rest. The updated lines are listed and the next batch can start.
